The six failed cross-repeat comparisons without checkpointing occur at alpha 0 and alpha 1, three failures each. The tolerance remains `rtol=2e-5, atol=2e-4`. Forward values, monthly states, log weights and ancestors agree exactly in all six setting/alpha combinations.

The diagnostic program's summary checked only the first cross pair in each combination; all six first pairs passed. Inspecting every retained repeat reveals additional failures, which are preserved here. The observed variation within each implementation means that a difference between the two implementations cannot yet be assigned to our new interface alone. The specific GPU operation responsible is not established.

This small-particle diagnosis does not approve formal optimization or establish J=5000 correctness. Disabling checkpointing has not been adopted as a fix. The previous failed GPU check remains part of the record, and no formal fit has been run.
