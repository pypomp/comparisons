# TFP GPU diagnosis

Job 65151858 ended with `FAILED 1:0` on an A40 at gl1524. Each of its three diagnostic subprocesses reached the 350-second limit. The partial outputs and terminal manifest were recovered and checked. This is an incomplete diagnostic run; no optimization was attempted.

The test uses the original Dacca model, 600 observation months, 5000 particles, one published parameter vector and one random seed. It compares the unchanged upstream `particle_filter` with our added `gradient_discount=1` argument. Both compute the same likelihood and its derivative through `GradientTape`. The saved increment arrays have 601 entries, including initialization.

| Mode | Saved original / modified evaluations | Likelihood increments | Gradient comparison |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Default, checkpoint enabled | 1 / 0 | No original/modified pair available | Insufficient output |
| Deterministic operations, checkpoint enabled | 4 / 3 | Exact in all 12 available cross pairs | Exact in all 12 cross pairs and all 9 within-function pairs |
| Default operations, checkpoint disabled | 4 / 2 | Exact in all 8 available cross pairs | No exact cross pair; maximum absolute difference 2.3842e-7 |

These pair counts reuse the saved evaluations; they are not independent replicates or extra runs. All saved arrays are finite. The intended fourth evaluation rebuilds the function's graph. It completed for the original function in the latter two modes, but not for the modified function. All three modes remain incomplete.

With checkpoint disabled, the unchanged original function also produced different gradients across its own repeated evaluations: none of its six pairs were identical, with the same maximum difference, 2.3842e-7. The modified function's two saved evaluations differed by at most 1.6391e-7. The largest original/modified relative L2 difference was 5.8893e-7. Thus exact gradient equality fails even within the original implementation in this setting. It is not a sufficient standalone test for attributing an error to our extension. The particular GPU operation responsible has not been identified.

The deterministic control agrees on the recorded full Dacca calculation. This observation does not certify every parameter value, the discounted derivative at alpha=0.97, or optimization behavior. The production configuration has not changed. The earlier exact-equality failure remains in the record, as do all three timeouts. The subprocess limits included imports, graph construction and evaluation; the saved records do not isolate the cause of the timeouts.

The audit verified 3090 referenced source files, 28 transferred files and 22 terminal-manifest entries. It recomputed all 12 stored array comparisons and every available original/modified cross pair from 14 NPZ files. [Audit summary](../results/tfp/gpu_diagnosis_20261101.json).

Slurm records 1203 GPU seconds for this job. Together with the earlier 432-second check, TFP validation has used 1635 GPU seconds of its 3600-second allowance. Subprocess times are nested within that allocation and are not added again. These measurements are diagnostic costs, not ordinary PF timings or fitting times.
